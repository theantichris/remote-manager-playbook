## Facilitation Notes (Optional)

Choose a format that works for the team:

- **Start, Stop, Continue**
- **Went Well, Could Improve**
- **Timeline Review**
- **Experiment Review**

Suggested timeboxes, adjustable to the discussion:

- Review previous actions: 5 minutes
- Silent reflection: 5 minutes
- Identify themes: 5 minutes
- Prioritize topics: 3 minutes
- Discuss and agree on improvements: 20 minutes

Track agreed improvements wherever the team manages its work, rather than letting them disappear into meeting notes.
