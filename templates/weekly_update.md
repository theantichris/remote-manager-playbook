# Weekly Update: {{title}}

A weekly update is an opportunity to reflect on what we've accomplished, what we've learned, what's challenging us, and where we might need help.

I don't want this to become another status report or an exercise in documenting every task we've completed.

Be honest, keep it useful, and focus on the things that matter. You don't need to answer every question every week. Share what you're comfortable sharing.

## Wins and Outcomes

**What felt like a win this week?**

- [Something that went well or made a difference.]

**What did we ship, accomplish, or improve?**

- [Meaningful outcomes, with links when useful.]

**How are the projects we're tracking progressing?**

- [Important changes, milestones, or outcomes.]

Keep detailed project updates in their existing source of truth. We shouldn't have to maintain the same status information in multiple places.

## Challenges and Friction

**What sucked this week?**

- [Something frustrating, difficult, or disappointing.]

**Is anything blocked or at risk?**

- [Blocker, impact, and what help is needed.]

**What felt like a poor use of your time?**

- [Unnecessary work, interruptions, meetings, or friction.]

**What's keeping you up at night?**

- [Concerns or uncertainties worth discussing, if any.]

These questions aren't about complaining. They're opportunities to identify problems we might be able to solve together.

## Decisions and Heads-Ups

**What decisions did we make this week?**

- [Decision, rationale, and supporting link.]

**Are there any heads-ups we should share?**

- [Changing priorities, upcoming challenges, dependencies, or information others should know.]

The goal is to make important context visible and avoid unnecessary surprises.

## Learning and Growth

**What did we learn this week?**

- [Something new, an experiment, a mistake, or an insight worth sharing.]

**What might we do differently next time?**

- [Opportunity to improve.]

**When were you at your happiest this week?**

- [Work, collaboration, or experiences that felt rewarding.]

I think it's important to understand not only what's difficult but also what people enjoy and find fulfilling.

## Looking Ahead

**What's most important next week?**

- [One or two priorities or outcomes.]

**What do you need from me or the team?**

- [Feedback, decisions, support, or help removing obstacles.]

**Is there anything we should discuss in our next 1:1?**

- [Topic worth a conversation.]

---

The purpose of a weekly update isn't to prove how busy we've been.

It's to recognize progress, surface challenges, share what we've learned, and understand how we can better support one another.

**A good weekly update should help us have fewer surprises, better conversations, and more meaningful progress.**
