# Strategy: {{title}}

A strategy explains the challenge we're addressing, the choices we're making, and the actions we'll take to achieve a meaningful outcome.

Keep it focused. The goal is to give people enough context to make aligned decisions without prescribing every detail.

## Guiding Policy

What approach will we take to address the challenge?

[Describe the principles and strategic choices that will guide our actions.]

Consider:

- Where will we focus our efforts?
- What advantages will we build on?
- What trade-offs are we willing to make?
- What constraints must we respect?
- How will this approach help us overcome the challenge?

The guiding policy should give people direction without becoming a detailed implementation plan.

## Reviewing and Adapting

When and how will we evaluate whether the strategy is working?

**Review cadence:** [When we'll assess progress]

During reviews, consider:

- Is our diagnosis still accurate?
- Are our assumptions holding up?
- Are the actions producing the intended outcomes?
- Have important constraints or opportunities changed?
- Do we need to adjust our approach?

We should be willing to change direction when evidence challenges our strategy without abandoning it simply because execution becomes difficult.
