# Recover the Reasoning Behind a Technical Decision

**Source:** Adapted from [How to Use AI to Become a More Effective Engineering Leader](https://newsletter.eng-leadership.com/p/how-to-use-ai-to-become-a-more-effective) by Gregor Ojstersek (October 20, 2026).

Replace bracketed placeholders with your context. Share only information you're permitted to provide to the AI tool, and verify information retrieved from external records.

~~~text
Search the available, authorized project records for the decision to [adopt technology or approach].

Find the relevant design discussions, documents, issues, and pull requests. Summarize the problem, alternatives considered, rationale for the choice, and any trade-offs or constraints recorded at the time.

Link to supporting sources. If the reasoning cannot be confirmed, say what is missing rather than guessing.
~~~
