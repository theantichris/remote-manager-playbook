# Course Notes

## Context

I am taking a course. Please summarize the transcript into structured, concise notes that capture the key learning material. Focus on definition, concpets, frameworks, and examples. Do not include filler or off topic remarks. Write in my own words rather than repeating the transcript verbatim.

## Topic Overview

What is the main subject or theme of the lesson/module? Capture the high level context.

## Key Concepts & Definitions

Capture important terms, definitions, and explanations. Use short, clear summaries.

## Frameworks & Methods

Note any structured methods, models, or step by step processes. Present them as lists, outlines, or diagrams.

## Examples & Case Studies

Note down any examples, case studies, or stories that help illustrate the concepts.

## Instructor Insights

Highlight any strong insights, advice, or memorable quotes that reinforce understanding.

## Applications & Takeaways

Summarize how these concepts can be applied in practice. Capture actionable insights or personal reflections.

## Questions for Review

Capture any open questions or prompts for deeper through, either from the instructor or ones I should follow up on.

## Next Steps

Note any suggested exercises, readings, or tasks to complete after this lesson.
