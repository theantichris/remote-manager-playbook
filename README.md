# Remote Manager Playbook

## Hey, y'all

Working with a remote, distributed team requires us to be intentional about how we share information. I believe in a handbook-first approach where information is documented, easy to find, and accessible to everyone who needs it. You shouldn't have to schedule a meeting or wait for someone to respond just to get the information you need.

Transparency matters to me. I want you to understand how I work, what I expect from you, and what you can expect from me. I believe we should be clear about our values, goals, and how we work together so nobody has to guess.

This playbook is a collection of living documents. I'll update it as I learn and as the way we work changes. If something is missing, outdated, or unclear, let me know. You're welcome to open an issue or PR, too.

## My Role

I am not here to make sure you do your job, I am here to make sure you can do your job.

My role is to help people, teams, and organizations achieve their goals and grow along the way. I want the people I work with to thrive both at work and outside of it. I want you to feel supported, fulfilled, and confident in your ability to do great work. That means helping you develop the skills, knowledge, and independence to succeed while making sure you have the support you need.

## What I Value Most

- **Holistic development.** We're not just employees, we're people. Our work and personal lives influence each other, and I believe both deserve our attention. I want you to succeed professionally without sacrificing what matters to you outside of work. Success in one part of life should help strengthen the other.
- **Growth mindset.** I want to see you grow in your role, your career, and as a person. Growth looks different for everyone, and it doesn't always mean pursuing a promotion. Let's work together to figure out where you want to go, set meaningful goals, and find opportunities to help you get there.
- **Outcomes and results.** I care more about the results we achieve than how busy we are. Let's start by understanding the outcome we're trying to achieve, then work backward to figure out the best way to get there together. We should be willing to experiment, learn, and adjust our approach along the way.
- **Collaboration.** We do our best work together. Share context, involve the right people, and work closely with teammates and partners to achieve our goals. I value independence, but that doesn't mean working in isolation. Good collaboration means keeping people informed, asking for help when needed, and helping others succeed.
- **Feedback.** I believe feedback is essential for growth, and it should go both ways. I want you to feel comfortable giving me honest feedback, even when it's difficult to hear. You don't need to wait for a 1:1 or a formal review. I welcome feedback publicly or privately, whichever you're most comfortable with. My goal is to create an environment where we can challenge each other, learn, and continuously improve.

## How I Operate

- My typical working hours are Monday through Friday, 08:00–16:00 Eastern. I turn off work notifications outside those hours because I believe in protecting personal time. I don't expect you to be available outside your normal working hours either. If something urgent comes up and you have my personal number, don't hesitate to text me.
- Organization is important to me. I rely heavily on my calendar, notes, and task lists to keep track of commitments and make sure things don't fall through the cracks. Writing things down helps me stay focused and follow through on what I've committed to.

## My Expectations

- **Keep your word.** If you commit to something, follow through. If things change or you can't meet a commitment, let me know early. Shit happens. Let's figure out what to do next together.
- **Be honest and transparent.** Say what you mean, share context, and don't hide problems or uncertainty. It's okay not to have all the answers. What's important is that we're open with each other so we can make good decisions together.
- **Continuously learn and grow.** Stay curious, ask questions, and look for opportunities to improve your skills and how we work. Share what you learn with others and be open to learning from them. I don't expect you to have all the answers, but I do expect you to be willing to learn and improve.
- **Focus.** Prioritize what matters most and limit how much work you take on at once. I believe we accomplish more by finishing important work than by starting more things. Protect your focus, be intentional about your priorities, and don't be afraid to push back when there's too much on your plate.
- **Maintain a healthy work-life cycle.** Your mental health, relationships, and the things you value outside work matter. Make time for them, respect your boundaries and those of others, and let me know when work is making that difficult. We'll figure out how to make things more sustainable together.

## Personality Quirks

- I'm quite forgetful, which is why I write everything down. If something isn't in my notes, calendar, or task list, there's a good chance I'll forget it. If we discuss something important and you don't see me capture it, please remind me.
- I have narcolepsy. If you see me yawning during a meeting, it doesn't mean I'm bored or uninterested. Likewise, if I get up and move around, it's because movement helps me stay alert and focused.
- I like to take time to think things through before responding or acting. If I go quiet during a conversation, I'm usually processing what we've discussed, not disengaged. I'll often say "processing..." so you know what's going on.

## How to Use This Playbook

- Skim the [index](INDEX.md) and jump to what you need now.
- Use it asynchronously. Link relevant documentation in issues, PRs, and conversations so people can find the context they need without having to ask for it.
- If you have questions or notice something missing or unclear, bring it up with me. If you'd like to suggest a change, feel free to open an issue or PR.

## Audience and Scope

- **Audience.** This playbook is for anyone who works with me, whether you report to me, work alongside me, or partner with my team.
- **Scope.** This playbook covers my approach to leadership, communication, strategy, product development, and team practices. It's intended to explain how I think and work, not prescribe how every team should operate. Technical documentation belongs with the code and systems it describes.
