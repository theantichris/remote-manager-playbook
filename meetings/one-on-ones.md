# 1:1s

I prefer to schedule 1:1s weekly, usually for 30 minutes. We can extend them when necessary, and we don't need to fill the entire slot. I try not to skip them unless one of us is on vacation. If a conflict comes up, let's reschedule rather than lose the time altogether.

This is time I reserve for **you**. It isn't a status meeting or a place for me to work through my task list. It's a chance for us to talk about what's going well, what's difficult, what you want to work toward, and how I can help.

## Shared Agenda

Whenever possible, we should have an agenda we can both add to before the meeting. Use whatever shared document works for the team. You don't need to wait until the meeting to add a question, a concern, or a topic you want to discuss.

I may bring topics too, but I want to make sure we get to what's important to you first.

## Before We Meet

1. Look at our previous notes and follow up on any action items.
1. Add topics or questions to the agenda.
1. Share anything that needs reading ahead of time.
1. Think about what you need from me, whether that's feedback, a decision, help removing a blocker, or just time to talk something through.

## The Conversation

### Personal Connection and Check-in

- How are you feeling about work and your workload?
- What's taking up the most mental space right now?
- Is there anything outside work affecting what you need from me? Share only what you're comfortable sharing.

### Your Agenda

- What's the most important thing for us to talk about today?
- Where are you feeling friction or uncertainty?
- What have you learned or accomplished that we should recognize?
- What help or decisions do you need?

### My Agenda

- Anything I need to clarify or follow up on.
- Cross-team collaboration or stakeholder alignment that affects your work.
- Career development, goals, and opportunities to grow.
- Feedback I owe you.

### Wrap-up

- What are we each taking away or committing to?
- Is there something we need more time to discuss?
- What could I do differently to support you?

## Between Meetings

We shouldn't save urgent problems or important feedback for the next 1:1. Reach out when you need me. We can work through routine updates asynchronously, but sensitive feedback or complicated discussions deserve a real conversation.

If we can't meet, add updates and questions to the agenda and we'll follow up on them directly. I aim to respond within one business day, and we'll handle time-sensitive issues sooner.

The [One-on-One Agenda](../templates/one-on-one-agenda.md) template is a starting point, not a script. The conversation should serve the person, not the template.
